# 💫 Stardust
Stardust currently adds 10 new vanilla-style enchantments to the game with even more to come. It aims to add variety to the slightly bland collection of enchantments currently in the game whilst still keeping it as close to vanilla as possible.

## Enchantments
| Name:         | Max: | Applicable on:     | Description:                                 |
|---------------|------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Electrocution | 2    | Sword              | Strikes lightnings into your enemy on hit.   |
| Frostbite     | 2    | Sword,             | Freezes the enemy on hit.                    |
| Decay         | 2    | Sword,             | Inflicts wither on the enemy on hit.         |
| Reach         | 3    | Any tool or weapon | Increases the reach distance of the tool.    |
| Frenzy        | 3    | Sword              | Increases the weapon's attack speed.         |
| Gaining       | 5    | Sword              | Increases experience yield from entities.    |
| Wisdom        | 5    | Pickaxe            | Increases experience yield from blocks.      |
| Pacing        | 3    | Boots              | Grants increased movement speed.             |
| Stability     | 3    | Boots              | Grants partial resistance against knockback. |
| Determination | 3    | Leggings           | Heals yourself faster when sneaking.         |
